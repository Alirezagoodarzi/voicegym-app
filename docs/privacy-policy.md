# VoiceGym Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

## Overview

VoiceGym ("we", "our", or "the app") is a voice-driven gym workout planner. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. We are committed to keeping your data minimal, transparent, and under your control.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect personal information.** VoiceGym does not require account creation, sign-up, or any form of registration.

The only data the app works with:

- **Voice commands** — spoken input captured locally on your device to parse exercise details. Voice audio is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing and is not stored permanently by us.
- **Exercise and workout data** — the exercises and workout plans you create are stored locally on your device only.
- **Optional profile data** — age, weight, and height entered in Settings are stored locally on your device only.
- **Preferences** — unit preferences and app settings are stored locally on your device only.

## How We Use Your Information

- Voice commands are used solely to parse exercise details (name, sets, reps, rest, weight) via AI.
- Workout and profile data is used solely to display and manage your workouts within the app.
- We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app's core features.

## Data Storage

All app data (workouts, profile, preferences) is stored exclusively in your device's local storage (`localStorage`). It never leaves your device except for voice commands sent to Anthropic's API for processing (see Third-Party Services below).

- No data is stored on our servers.
- No cloud sync in the free tier.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

## Third-Party Services (Anthropic Claude API)

VoiceGym uses the **Anthropic Claude API** to interpret your voice commands and parse them into structured exercise data.

- When you use a voice command, the transcribed text is sent to Anthropic's servers for processing.
- Anthropic's data handling is governed by their own Privacy Policy: [https://www.anthropic.com/privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)
- We do not send any personal profile data (age, weight, height) to Anthropic.
- Voice audio is processed on-device by your browser's Web Speech API before any text is sent to Anthropic.

## Voice Data

- Voice audio is captured and transcribed entirely on your device using the browser's built-in Web Speech API.
- Only the resulting text transcript of your voice command is sent to Anthropic's Claude API.
- No audio recordings are stored or transmitted by VoiceGym.
- Transcripts are processed in real time and not retained after the exercise is parsed.

## Children's Privacy

VoiceGym is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
