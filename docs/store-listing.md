## Full Description (4000 chars max)

**VoiceGym — Your AI-Powered Voice Workout Planner**

Stop typing. Start training. VoiceGym lets you build your workout plan the way you actually think — by speaking it out loud.

**How It Works**

Simply tap the microphone and say your exercise in natural language:

*"Bench press on barbell, 4 sets, 8 reps, 90 seconds rest, 80 kg"*

VoiceGym's AI instantly parses your command and adds it to your workout plan — no tapping, no typing, no friction.

---

**Key Features**

🎙 **Voice-First Input**
Speak your exercises naturally. AI handles the rest. Supports partial commands too — just say "sets 4 reps 8" to update specific fields.

🤖 **AI-Powered Parsing**
Powered by Anthropic Claude, one of the most advanced AI models available. Understands flexible, natural language voice input with high accuracy.

📋 **Smart Workout Planner**
Build your full workout plan with exercises, sets, reps, rest times, and weight. Drag to reorder exercises however you like.

⏱ **Live Workout Session**
Start a session from your plan. Track each set with a tap, monitor your rest timer, and see your progress in real time.

📊 **Session History**
Every completed workout is saved. Review past sessions by date, duration, and completion rate.

⚖️ **kg / lbs Support**
Toggle between kilograms and pounds. Set your preferred unit in settings.

👤 **Personal Profile**
Store your age, weight, and height for future features like volume tracking and personalized recommendations.

✏️ **Full Manual Control**
Prefer typing? Add and edit exercises manually with intuitive stepper inputs for all numeric fields.

---

**Privacy Focused**

- No account required
- All workout data stored locally on your device
- Voice commands processed in real time — not stored permanently
- No ads, no data selling, no tracking

---

**Free to Use**

VoiceGym is completely free. Your workouts, your data, your device.
